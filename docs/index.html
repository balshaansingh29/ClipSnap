Two Essential Utilities in Harmony

Clean up your menu bar without sacrificing any productivity.

📋

Smart Clipboard History

Never lose a snippet again. Search, preview, and paste rich content with seamless keyboard shortcuts.

  • ✓ Instant Fuzzy Search: Type anything to filter text, RTF, and image clips in real-time.
  • ✓ Quick Paste: Press ⌘1–⌘9 to paste items directly into your active app.
  • ✓ Plain Text Paste: Strip annoying formatting anytime using ⌥↵.
  • ✓ Password Protection: Automatically ignores 1Password, Bitwarden, and sensitive apps.
🪟

Intuitive Window Snapping

Tile and organize your windows exactly where you want them with natural gestures and shortcuts.

  • ✓ Cycle Sizing: Repeat half or third shortcuts to cycle between ½ → ⅔ → ⅓.
  • ✓ Shake to Snap: Gently wiggle your cursor while dragging any window to summon a layout palette!
  • ✓ Edge & Corner Snapping: Drag windows to any display border with live visual previews.
  • ✓ Multi-Display Ready: Perfectly handles secondary displays and custom menu bar heights.

Meet Snip 🐾

Utilities don't have to feel boring. Snip is an interactive, 100% code-rendered companion living in your popover. Snip winks when you copy, rests peacefully when you pause monitoring, and cheers on your daily productivity.
